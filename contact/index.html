Metro

Simplest way to arrive is via the Metro. The Johns Hopkins stop is steps away from the lab. The stop is on Broadway St.

Parking and arrival

Caroline Street Garage is located off North Caroline Street, between East Monument Street and McElderry Street. Vehicle entrances are on East Monument Street and McElderry Street.

Please note: Johns Hopkins identifies Caroline Street Garage primarily as employee parking, with visitor parking for oversized vehicles on the first level. Visitors without parking authorization should consider the McElderry Street Garage, which offers regular visitor parking.

Parking access and hours can change. See Johns Hopkins' official garage information or call the parking office at 410-955-5333 before visiting.
