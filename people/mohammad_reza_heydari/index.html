Research

Mohammad Reza joined the lab in 2025 as a graduate student in the BME PhD Program. He received a BS in Electrical Engineering from Sharif University of Technology, followed by an MS from Sharif. During his bachelor and masters studies he was ranked 1st and 2nd among his peers. He designed an algorithm to decode the mossy fiber input to the cerebellum and discovered that in lobules VI and VII of the vermis, this input encoded both copies of commands to the eyes, as well as to the tongue. He designed a robotic system to train our marmosets to use their tongue to target a location for harvesting food. He is working on design of experiments that ask how the cerebellum learns internal models of tongue control.
