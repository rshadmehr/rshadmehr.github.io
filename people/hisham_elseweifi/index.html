Research

Hisham was an undergraduate at the Biomedical Engineering program at the Univ. of Ilinois (Chicago), where he graduted with high honors (summa cum laude). He joined the Biomedical Engineering PhD program in 2024 and was selected for an NIH training fellowship in neuroengineering. Hisham designed a method to find potent vectors for Purkinje cells of the cerebellum. This is a method with which one can estimate the vector of change in behavior caused by spikes in a neuron. This led to the discovery that the downstream effects of two Purkinje cells can be understood as superposition of their potent vectors.
