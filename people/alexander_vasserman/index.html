Research

Sasha joined the lab in 2024 as a graduate student in the BME PhD Program. He received a BS and MD in Biomedical Engineering from Drexel University. Sasha's research focuses on how the cerebellum contributes to control of the tongue. His goal is to develop the first primate behavioral task specifically designed to induce sensory prediction errors during targeted licking and conduct the first analysis of climbing fiber tuning and P-cell computations during this task. His work will bridge the gap in our understanding of cerebellar control over tongue movements and will aid lingual neuroscience by developing a new non-human primate model in marmosets. He was awarded an NIH NRSA Fellowship in 2025.
