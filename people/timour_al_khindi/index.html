Research

Tim graduated from University of Toronto with a BS in Psychology and Neuroscience. He then joined the MD/PhD program at Johns Hopkins University, where he was awarded the 2012 William C. Walker Award for psychiatry research, followed by the International Student Research Fellowship, Howard Hughes Medical Institute. He completed a PhD in Neuroscience with Prof. Alex Kolodkin and then the MD program. He was then a resident in the neurosurgery program at Johns Hopkins, where he was awarded the Harvey Cushing Medical Student Hunterian Research Award. Tim's research focuses on real-time decoding of neuronal activity in the non-human primate cerebellum, aiming to develop a brain machine interface for the cerebellum.
