Research

Alden is a graduate student in the Biomedical Engineering PhD program at Johns Hopkins University. Her research focuses on understanding how the cerebellum contributes to the control of movement.

She studies cerebellar control of saccadic eye movements in marmosets, combining neurophysiological recordings with computational approaches to understand how populations of cerebellar neurons influence behavior.
