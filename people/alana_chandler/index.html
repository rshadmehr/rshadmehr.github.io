Research

Alana graduated from Univ. of Maryland with a B.S. in Bioengineering, where she was a Philip Merril Presidential Scholar. She was then enrolled in the Johns Hopkins MD/PhD Program and was awarded the Pardoll MSTP Scholarship, and subsequently the David Yue Scholarship. She joined our lab in October of 2024. She is interested in the neural basis of learning, including the role of the climbing fibers of the cerebellum as a teacher and organizer of the Purkinje cells and the molecular layer interneurons. Alana was awarded an NIH NRSA Fellowship to support her PhD training and research.
