Research

Nazanin joined the lab in 2023 as a PhD student in the Biomedical Engineering Program. She is a graduate of Amirkabir University of Technology, where she was ranked 2nd in her graduating class with a BS in Biomedical Engineering. She was trained in the National Organization for Development of Exceptional Talent in Iran. She is interested in brain-machine interface development. Nazanin led the development of microstimulation of the cerebellum in our lab using multi-contact silicon probes. This produced the ability to simutaneously stimulate a small region and record from the neurons in that region. She discovered that following brief microstimulation the Purkinje cells experience climbing fiber input, suggesting a mechanism for exogenous induction of plasticity and learning.
