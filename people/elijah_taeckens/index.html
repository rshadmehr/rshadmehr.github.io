Research

Eli Tackens graduated with a BS in Electrical Engineering from the University of Maryland, then joined our laboratory in 2024 as a graduate student in the BME PhD Program. He won the UMD ASPIRE Outstanding Student Research Award, the Suhon Guha Memorial Award for best senior research thesis, and was selected for a Neuroengineering Fellowship at Johns Hopkins. He was awarded an NIH NRSA Fellowship to support his PhD training at Johns Hopkins. Eli is examining the function of the cerebellum during cognitively challenging behaviors such as control of impulsivity and planning. He is first in our lab to record from the lateral cerebellum, regions that connect to the cerebral cortex.
