Research

Joey graduated Magna Cum Laude from University of Washington with a BS in Bioengineering. As an undergraduate, he used cEEG datasets to predict seizures and proposed research for maladaptive myelination in epilepsy and advances in optogenetic inhibition of oligodendrocytes. At Johns Hopkins he has designed a robotic system for control of dispensing of rewards to marmosets. This robotic system allows for precise positioning of food rewards, making goal-directed tongue movements to variable targets.
